## Overview

Airbnb remains the reference for product clarity: a marketplace that makes browsing, comparing, and booking feel immediate on mobile. FreeTourPraga should borrow that level of simplicity, not its brand. The interface must feel like a boutique Italian travel service in Prague: local, protective, operationally precise, and easy to trust before money changes hands.

The base canvas is **Sky Canvas** (`{colors.canvas}` - #F4F9FF), with white cards and a white top navigation. **Vltava Blue** (`{colors.navy}` - #1268D6) carries brand, navigation, links, iconography, footer and trust-heavy sections without the visual weight of the previous near-black navy. **Prague Orange** (`{colors.primary}` - #FF8A00) is the single conversion color. It is intentionally scarce: primary CTAs, booking confirmation, payment actions, selected calendar states where conversion is implied, and critical commercial badges.

Type uses **Inter** or the system stack instead of Airbnb Cereal. The hierarchy is slightly firmer than Airbnb because FreeTourPraga sells reassurance, not only discovery. Display text is confident but not oversized. The site should still trust photography, tour cards, meeting-point maps, review signals, and booking surfaces to carry the experience.

The shape language remains friendly and consumer-grade: 8px buttons, 12px tour cards, pill-shaped search/booking selectors, and circular icon buttons. Corners are slightly less soft than Airbnb to avoid clone behavior and to better match a professional tour operator.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Airbnb-inspired usability, FreeTourPraga-owned identity: clear marketplace patterns, but different palette, typography, copy, content hierarchy, and trust signals.
- Bright blue brand frame: navigation text, footer, institutional sections, booking reassurance, links and admin states use `{colors.navy}`.
- Prague Orange conversion moments: `{colors.primary}` is reserved for "Prenota Ora", "Conferma", "Paga", "Invia Richiesta B2B", and selected/high-intent states.
- Mobile-first booking: 85%+ expected mobile usage means tour cards, booking selectors, payment choice, WhatsApp support, and calendar flows must be one-hand usable.
- Protection-first content: anti-truffe, cambio sicuro, gruppi ristretti, assistenza WhatsApp H24, cancellazione/modifica autonoma, and guide locali are product signals, not decorative marketing copy.
- Photo-led Prague experience: real Prague/tour imagery, meeting points, maps, guide portraits, and customer trust signals should replace generic stock travel imagery.
- Contract-aware scope: Fase 1 focuses on site, tour admin, booking, Stripe/payment link, emails, calendar and deploy; Fase 2 expands into Master Inventory, self-service, affiliates, OTA parsing, WhatsApp reminders, Ads/KPI.

## Colors

### Brand & Accent
- **Prague Orange** (`{colors.primary}` - #FF8A00): The single primary conversion color. Use for booking CTAs, payment CTAs, selected commercial actions, and high-intent badges. Do not use it for decorative text.
- **Orange Active** (`{colors.primary-active}` - #E87500): Press and hover state for primary conversion actions.
- **Orange Disabled** (`{colors.primary-disabled}` - #FFC46B): Disabled or pending primary actions. Pair with dark blue text, not white.

The system uses **Inter** as the primary typeface, with system fallbacks. This keeps the interface modern, readable, and easy to implement without licensing custom fonts. It deliberately avoids Airbnb Cereal to keep the reference inspirational rather than copied.

There is no separate display family. Trust comes from clear hierarchy, stable spacing, quality photography, and precise operational copy.

### Principles

Display type should be direct and reassuring. The main hero can use the strategic promise: "Goditi Praga con gli occhi, non con lo schermo del telefono. Al resto pensiamo noi." Supporting copy should carry the operational proof: gruppi ristretti, guide italiane/locali, prenotazione rapida, cancellazione flessibile, WhatsApp H24.

Avoid oversized editorial typography inside booking, admin, and operational surfaces. These are workflow areas, so labels, totals, dates, and status indicators must scan quickly.

### Note on Font Substitutes

If Inter is unavailable, use the system stack. Do not substitute with Airbnb Cereal or Circular unless the user explicitly licenses and requests those fonts.

## Layout

### Spacing System

- **Base unit:** 4px, with a 2px micro-step.
- **Tokens:** `{spacing.xxs}` 2px, `{spacing.xs}` 4px, `{spacing.sm}` 8px, `{spacing.md}` 12px, `{spacing.base}` 16px, `{spacing.lg}` 24px, `{spacing.xl}` 32px, `{spacing.xxl}` 48px, `{spacing.section}` 72px.
- **Section padding:** 72px for major public bands; 48px for dense operational/admin surfaces.
- **Card padding:** 24px for booking/reservation panels, 16px for tour cards, 12px for dense admin table rows.
- **Gutters:** 16px between tour cards on mobile/tablet, 24px on desktop.

### Grid & Container

- **Max content width:** 1200-1280px for public pages.
- **Homepage:** Airbnb-like card clarity, but content order must support FreeTourPraga's funnel: hero/search, trust strip, featured tours, anti-stress/anti-truffa pillars, reviews, B2B/partner entry, FAQ.
- **Tour listing:** Responsive card grid with filters for date, category, duration, price, and availability.
- **Tour detail:** Two-column desktop layout with story/content/media on the left and sticky booking card on the right. On mobile the booking CTA becomes sticky at the bottom.
- **Admin/back-office:** Dense but quiet operational layout: sidebar/topbar, calendar, booking table, tour editor, payment status, manual booking insertion.
- **Footer:** Navy surface with service links, legal links, contact, WhatsApp, social, and partner/B2B access.

### Whitespace Philosophy

Public pages should feel calm and protected, not empty. The first viewport must quickly communicate Prague, Italian support, low-friction booking, and WhatsApp protection. Below the fold, density can increase through tour cards, trust facts, and operational details.

Admin pages should prioritize scanning and repeated work. Use compact spacing, stable table rows, clear filters, and status chips. Do not apply marketing-style hero layouts to internal tools.

## Elevation

The system uses one restrained shadow tier plus flat surfaces.

- **Flat:** White navbar, bright blue footer, editorial bands, legal areas and admin shell backgrounds.
- **Card shadow:** `box-shadow: rgba(20, 50, 75, 0.06) 0 1px 2px, rgba(18, 104, 214, 0.10) 0 8px 24px` for booking cards, dropdowns, sticky mobile booking bars, and hovered tour cards.
- **Modal scrim:** `{colors.scrim}` rendered at 50% opacity.

Avoid heavy luxury shadows, glassmorphism, gradient blobs, and decorative depth. Trust should come from clarity, not visual effects.

## Components

### Buttons

**`button-primary`** - Prague Orange fill, dark blue text, 8px radius, 48px height. Use only for conversion: "Prenota Ora", "Conferma Prenotazione", "Paga con Stripe", "Invia Richiesta B2B".

**`button-primary-active`** - Darker orange press state. No bounce or dramatic transform.

**`button-primary-disabled`** - Pale orange for unavailable or incomplete flows. Must include clear helper text explaining what is missing.

**`button-secondary`** - White card surface with dark blue text and 1px blue-tinted border. Use for "Dettagli", "Modifica", "Salva bozza", "Contatta su WhatsApp" when not the primary CTA.

**`button-tertiary-text`** - Plain text action for low-priority links such as "Leggi di più", "Vedi policy", "Annulla".

**`button-pill-primary`** - Pill-shaped Prague Orange action for compact commercial prompts, such as "Vedi disponibilita", "Richiedi preventivo", or "Apri WhatsApp" when it is the main action in a small surface.

### Search Surface

**`search-bar-pill`** - Airbnb-inspired booking/search surface adapted to tours: "Tour", "Data", "Partecipanti", "Lingua" or "Categoria". White surface, pill radius, 64px desktop height, collapsed single-tap mobile entry.

**`search-orb`** - Circular orange action button at the end of the search surface. Icon should be search or arrow-right depending on context.

### Top Navigation

**`top-nav`** - White surface, 80px desktop height, subtle blue-tinted divider and shadow. Brand left, concise blue nav center/right: Tour, Tour privati, Scuole e gruppi, Partner Europa, FAQ/Contatti. Mobile collapses to logo + outlined blue menu + sticky WhatsApp affordance.

**`product-tab-active`** - Active nav item uses deep blue text and a small orange underline.

**`product-tab-inactive`** - Vltava Blue text on white; hover moves to the deeper blue.

**`new-tag`** - Amber pill for "H24", "POCHI POSTI", "B2B", or new tour labels. Do not use Airbnb-style "NEW" unless content is actually newly launched.

### Listing Cards

**`property-card`** - Reinterpreted as `tour-card`: photo-first card with tour title, duration, meeting area, starting price, rating/review count, next availability, and primary booking affordance. Keep the Airbnb clarity of image + compact metadata, but use Prague-specific content.

**`property-card-photo`** - Tour photo plate. Prefer real tour/location images: Ponte Carlo, Castello, Mala Strana, Citta Vecchia, birrerie storiche, guide in context.

**`experience-card`** - Premium or thematic tour card: Castello, Birra, Fantasmi, tour privati, scuole. Taller image ratio allowed for editorial emphasis.

**`guest-favorite-badge`** - Reinterpreted as trust/availability badge: "Gruppo ristretto", "Guida italiana", "Cancellazione flessibile", "Anti-truffa inclusa".

### Listing Detail

**`rating-display-card`** - Trust block for review rating, number of ospiti accompagnati, response time WhatsApp, or no-show reduction KPI. Use sparingly.

**`amenity-row`** - Tour inclusions and reassurance rows: guida italiana, punto incontro geolocalizzato, pagamento online o contanti, assistenza WhatsApp, gruppo a numero chiuso.

**`reviews-card`** - Customer review excerpts. Favor Italian traveler concerns: chiarezza, puntualita, sicurezza, bambini/famiglie, organizzazione.

**`host-card`** - Guide/operator card. Shows guide name, language, local experience, contact route, and operational notes. Avoid Airbnb host terminology in UI copy.

**`reservation-card`** - Sticky booking card with date, slot, participants by age band, price rules, payment choice, discount logic for multiple tours, and booking summary. In Fase 1 it can generate a Stripe payment link or allow cash on site, per contract. In Fase 2 it must respect Master Inventory and self-service modification/cancellation.

### Date Picker

**`date-picker-day`** - 40x40px day cell. Shows available, low availability, unavailable, and selected states.

**`date-picker-day-selected`** - Vltava Blue selected state with white text. Use orange only for the final conversion action, not every selection, unless the selection is inside a CTA-like booking step.

### Forms

**`text-input`** - White surface, 1px slate border, 8px radius, 56px height. Labels are clear and operational: nome, email, telefono, numero partecipanti, note logistiche, hotel, richiesta B2B.

Error states must be explicit and practical: "Inserisci un numero WhatsApp valido" is better than generic "Campo non valido".

### Footer

**`footer-light`** - Despite the legacy name, this is a Vltava Blue footer. It should include contact, WhatsApp, legal, social, partner/B2B, useful Prague safety links, and essential trust statements.

**`legal-band`** - Deeper blue legal strip for privacy, terms, business identity, VAT/registration details, and provider cost disclaimers where needed.

## Responsive Behavior

| Name | Width | Key Changes |
|---|---:|---|
| Mobile | < 744px | Navy nav collapses to logo/menu; booking/search becomes a single compact pill or sticky bottom CTA; tour cards stack 1-up; WhatsApp floating button is always reachable; reservation card becomes sticky bottom summary. |
| Tablet | 744-1128px | Tour cards 2-up; booking card can sit below media or in a narrower right rail; filters become horizontal scroll or drawer. |
| Desktop | 1128-1440px | Full nav, hero/search surface, tour cards 3-4-up, tour detail 2-column with sticky booking card. |
| Wide | > 1440px | Content remains capped around 1280px; avoid stretching cards or hero text excessively. |

### Touch Targets

- Primary CTAs minimum 48x48px.
- WhatsApp floating action minimum 56x56px on mobile.
- Calendar day cells minimum 40x40px.
- Participant steppers minimum 44x44px.
- Admin check-in actions minimum 44px tall for guide smartphone use.

### Collapsing Strategy

- Top nav collapses into a menu sheet below 744px.
- Booking/search segments collapse into a single entry point that opens a full-screen mobile booking sheet.
- Sticky booking bottom bar appears on tour detail pages after the user scrolls past the hero.
- Admin/guide check-in views should collapse to task-first lists: today, tour, slot, customer, present/absent.

## Known Gaps

- **Content inventory:** Final tour names, prices, durations, meeting points, caps, age bands, images, and descriptions must be supplied before visual QA.
- **Booking rules:** Cancellation, modification, refund, cash payment, Stripe payment link, discount, and age-band pricing rules need final approval during Step 1.
- **Inventory scope:** Fase 1 needs at least basic availability control; full Master Inventory and OTA reconciliation are Fase 2 per contract.
- **WhatsApp scope:** Fase 1 can include persistent WhatsApp links/widgets. Automated WhatsApp reminders are Fase 2 and likely require Twilio or equivalent.
- **ERP scope:** Strategic document mentions advanced margin and payout reporting, but the contract only clearly covers affiliate/promoter tracking and report commissions in Fase 2. Treat full ERP as a later specification unless separately approved.
- **OTA parsing:** Civitatis/GetYourGuide email parsing depends on email formats and third-party availability. The contract already notes this dependency.
- **Legal/fiscal copy:** GDPR, cancellation terms, invoices/receipts, Czech/Italian business details, and B2B terms require final legal/accounting input.
